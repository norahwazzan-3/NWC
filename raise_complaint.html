Water Consumption Awareness

Check for hidden leaks – a dripping tap wastes up to 15 liters/day.

Monitor your usage and compare meter readings regularly.

Use water-efficient fixtures to save up to 50% water.

Turn off taps while brushing teeth or washing dishes.

Only run full loads in your washing machine and dishwasher.

Water gardens early morning or late evening to reduce evaporation.

Reuse rainwater or rinse water for cleaning or watering plants.

Maintain proper water pressure to avoid pipe damage and waste.

Understand your water bill – check for unexpected changes.

Before complaining, ensure there is no internal leak or extra consumption.
